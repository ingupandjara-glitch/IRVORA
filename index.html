FAQ

Common questions

How long does delivery take? +
Flyer Design is delivered within 24 hours. All other services are delivered within 2–3 working days from when we receive everything we need from you.
How do I upload my documents? +
After checkout you'll be taken to a secure upload page where you can attach PDF, DOC, DOCX, PNG or JPEG files up to 20MB each.
Can I request revisions? +
Yes. Every order includes a revision round — just reply to your delivery email or use your customer dashboard to request changes.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay via Stripe. PayPal support is planned.
Do you offer refunds? +
If work hasn't started, you can cancel for a full refund. Once a specialist has begun your order, please see our Refund Policy for what applies.
