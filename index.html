THE FINDING
The problem

The file is the finding.

Inspectors don't fail care. They fail evidence. Four rules now decide what your staff files must show:

MAR 2025

Care Certificate went 15 → 16 standards

The updated Certificate added a 16th standard. Inspectors now check induction files against it — a file built on the old 15 is already out of date.

ACT 2022

Oliver McGowan training is a legal requirement

Mandatory under the Health and Care Act 2022, with the Code of Practice in force since September 2025. Every CQC-registered provider must evidence it, tiered by role.

REG 19 SCH 3

Fit-and-proper evidence must exist on paper

Qualifications, references, DBS dates, right to work. If it isn't evidenced in the file, it didn't happen — that's the inspection standard, not ours.

REG 18

Training and supervision must be evidenced, not asserted

Supervision dates, appraisal dates, training expiry. "We do it regularly" scores nothing. Dates in a file score everything.
