# Legal Page Templates — CareerForge

**Important:** These are starting templates for a UK digital career-services business, written to align with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, PECR, and UK consumer law as understood at the time of writing. They are **not legal advice**. Have a qualified UK solicitor review and sign off on all six pages before publishing, and replace every `[bracketed]` placeholder with your real business details.

## 1. Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [date]

CareerForge ("we", "us") provides digital career services (CV writing, LinkedIn optimisation, cover letters, and design services). This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and your rights.

### What we collect
- Contact details you give us: name, email, phone number.
- Order details: service purchased, additional notes.
- Files you upload: CVs, photos, logos, LinkedIn URLs, and similar documents needed to deliver your order.
- Payment confirmation data from our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full card numbers ourselves.
- Technical data: IP address, browser type, and cookie data (see our [Cookie Policy](cookie-policy.html)).

### Why we process it (lawful basis)
- **Contract** — to deliver the service you purchased.
- **Legal obligation** — to meet tax, accounting and consumer-protection requirements.
- **Consent** — for optional marketing communications and non-essential cookies.
- **Legitimate interests** — to prevent fraud and keep the service secure.

### How long we keep it
Order and payment records are kept for [6 years] to meet UK tax obligations. Uploaded files are deleted [90 days] after order completion unless you ask us to keep them. Contact-form enquiries that don't lead to an order are deleted after [12 months].

### Who we share it with
Payment processing (Stripe), transactional email (e.g. Resend), and file storage (e.g. Supabase/AWS S3) providers, each bound by their own data-processing agreements. We do not sell personal data.

### Your rights
Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete, or export your data, or object to certain processing. Contact [privacy@yourdomain.co.uk]. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

### Data deletion & export
Request account and data deletion, or a copy of your data, at any time via [privacy@yourdomain.co.uk] or your customer dashboard. We aim to action requests within 30 days.

### Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to uploaded files is restricted to staff working on your order.

## 3. Refund Policy

**Last updated:** [date]

- **Before work starts:** full refund available on request.
- **After work starts, before delivery:** partial refund at our discretion, reflecting work already completed.
- **After delivery:** we'll fix genuine errors or omissions against your original brief via the included revision round. Refunds after delivery are considered case-by-case and are not automatic, in line with the Consumer Rights Act 2015 — services must be carried out with reasonable care and skill, and we'll put things right if they weren't.
- **How to request:** email [support@yourdomain.co.uk] with your order reference.
- Refunds are returned to the original payment method via Stripe, typically within 5–10 business days.
