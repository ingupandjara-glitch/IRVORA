Questions managers actually ask

Is my staff data safe?

Yes — structurally, not just by promise. The checker runs entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded, sent or saved, and we could not see your file if we tried. On the Rescue call we work screen-shared, so the file never leaves your machine. We never ask you to email us a tracker.

Will this guarantee our CQC rating?

No — and nobody honest sells that. We get your records inspection-ready: gaps found, evidence chased, audit trail dated. The inspection itself belongs to CQC.

What if we have 100+ staff?

Fine — the checker takes one row per person. On the call we work through the red flags in priority order; extra time beyond 90 minutes is £49 per 30 minutes, agreed with you before it starts, never sprung after.

We don't have a tracker at all.

Start with the free template inside the checker — Name, role, start date, DBS date, right to work, references, Care Certificate, Oliver McGowan tier, training expiry, supervision and appraisal dates. Fill what you know; the checker tells you what's missing.
